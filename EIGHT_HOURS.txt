EIGHT HOURS ON SITE: one block per hour, each with a stop rule
EVENT_DAY.txt has the checklist steps. DECISION_CARD.txt has the fixes. This plan uses both.
Laptop commands run in the program folder. Board commands run in the board's program folder.

Rules for every hour.
- The event pilot flies the aircraft. Team members do not fly. Where a step says "fly", give the request to the pilot.
- Make one change at a time. Fly, record, look at the log, then change.
- Keep a copy of every settings file that flew well: cp settings.json good_HHMM.json.
- Half of each hour is spare time. Use it for the fallback, not for new ideas.
- The rules at the top of EVENT_DAY.txt apply to every hour: propellers off with a cable, battery power for the
  board, antennas before power, the VTx (video transmitter) limit, no flight with gusts of 46 km/h or more.
- Cable and propellers for each flight (EVENT_DAY step 7): start the program with the cable on and the propellers
  off (tools/deploy.sh). Unplug the cable. Then install the propellers; the battery stays connected.
  After landing: the pilot disarms, remove the propellers, then connect the cable.
- A battery change switches the board off, and the program with it. Start it again before the next flight
  (EVENT_DAY step 7, items 1 to 3). (tools/deploy.sh boot-on starts it at each power-on instead. Not rehearsed.
  Then stop it before check, mask, calib or bench.)
- Storage: the program records raw pictures the whole time it runs, also on the ground. After landing, stop it
  and pull the logs: on the laptop, tools/deploy.sh stop, then tools/deploy.sh pull.
  No SD card: then delete the raw files on the board (rm <log_dir>/raw_*.npz). The recorder stops for the rest of
  the run when the free space falls under min_free_gb.
- Before the day: rehearse hours 5 and 6 on the laptop with tools/sim_rec.py --video JUSTICE_D and
  tools/replay_tune.py. Done 2026-11-20: integ/rehearsal_2026-11-20/README.txt.

BEFORE HOUR 1. STAGE 2, ROLES, FIRST CONTACT (20 to 30 min, the whole team)
(The roles, the Stage 2 list and the first question list come from Aydan's TOMORROW.md, branch origin/spec-justice-d.)
1. Read Stage 2 together. Write each answer next to its question in ORGANIZER_QUESTIONS.md: the firmware list, the
   board access, the camera and its time stamps, a rangefinder, the altitude levels, the hold rule, the points and
   bonus routes, the evidence rules, the flight booking.
2. Book the pilot at once. Book two things: first 10 minutes with propellers off for the takeover demonstration
   (PILOT_SAFETY.txt), then the first hover (hour 4). The pilot must see the takeover demonstration before the
   pilot flies our hold.
3. Ask the pilot and the organizers the open questions of ORGANIZER_QUESTIONS.md, in their order.
4. Choose the firmware. Prefer ArduPilot when it is offered: our ArduCopter simulator runs Orqa's branch.
5. Choose the GNSS level: DECISION_CARD row 45. No GNSS from power-on is the default, and only that level keeps
   the bonus route "GNSS-free start" open. gnss_setup (GNSS before the hold only) is permitted for a scored hold.
   gnss_fallback is for practice flights. Ask the crew first if the aircraft gets GNSS at the event.
6. On the laptop, apply the profiles: python3 tools/use_profile.py apply outdoor_day goal_a <px4|ardupilot> justice_d
   (No SD card: add no_sd last.) EVENT_DAY step 0 has the details.
7. Decide now which flights are hold-out flights (DATA_SPLITS.md). Keep the last ladder flights for the result.
   Do not tune on them.
8. Open HARDWARE_BRINGUP.md. One person writes each measured value there, with the time and the log file.
Roles (change them to the team size):
- Lead: reads Stage 2, decides the firmware and the GNSS level, talks to the organizers and the pilot, owns the clock.
- Board: ssh, board_check, check, deploy, pull.
- Calibration: mask, calib with a hand turn (EVENT_DAY step 4), bench. A checkerboard only in the development area.
- Analyst: plots, timing, the tables of EVIDENCE.md.
- Scribe and presenter: writes the time of each action and result, fills HARDWARE_BRINGUP.md and the [TODAY] slots
  of PRESENTATION.md and of the slides.
STOP RULE: the firmware is chosen, the pilot is booked, and each open question has an answer or an owner.

HOUR 1 (0:00 to 1:00). Board, Python, link.
1. Remove the propellers. Attach the antennas. Connect the drone battery: it powers the board. The laptop USB-C port
   cannot power the flight controller (APB manual).
   Connect the laptop to the USB-C port of the i.MX. The port is a network device. The board address is 192.168.75.1.
   Type: ssh root@192.168.75.1
   If ssh fails, the APB manual gives three other ways in:
   a. A USB-Ethernet adapter: the board has the static address 192.168.1.75.
   b. The serial console on UART2 (JST connector).
   c. Recovery mode: telnet 192.168.75.1, user root, no password.
2. Write one line into tools/deploy.env: BOARD=192.168.75.1.
3. On the laptop: do EVENT_DAY step 0 (profiles, log_dir). Its last item copies the program to the board.
4. On the board: sh tools/board_check.sh
5. For each FAIL line, run the fix line under it. The wheels are on the USB stick (tools/wheels/README.txt).
6. Read the new board_check.sh lines: /dev/video2 and /dev/video3, the pixel formats, /dev/ttymxc2, the IIO devices,
   the board temperature, the clock, /sdcard, and the OpenCV build (V4L2 and GStreamer).
7. Check the clock. The board clock is wrong after the first start until the board gets network time.
   tools/deploy.sh sets the board clock from the laptop at each copy and start, and prints both clocks.
   If the clock line still shows a year before 2026, connect the network. Or type: date -s 'YYYY-MM-DD hh:mm'
8. Select the flight controller route on the board: sh tools/board_check.sh --route. It runs these two commands:
   gpioset -t0 -c gpiochip3 13=1
   gpioset -t0 -c gpiochip3 0=0
   (The APB manual gives these commands for UART3, /dev/ttymxc2. It gives no baud rate and no protocol.)
9. Check the temperature: cat /sys/class/thermal/thermal_zone0/temp. The value is in millidegrees C. The board stops at 95 C.
10. Do EVENT_DAY step 1 and step 2.
STOP RULE: board_check.sh shows ALL PASS. The firmware line shows px4 or ardupilot.
If the firmware is not px4 or ardupilot: stop. The program cannot fly it (EVENT_DAY step 1).
Betaflight or INAV: see research/betaflight_route.md.
If pip fails: use the last part of step 3 in tools/wheels/README.txt (unpack the wheels).

HOUR 2 (1:00 to 2:00). Profile, parameters, mask.
1. On the laptop: python3 tools/use_profile.py show. Check the profile names of hour 1 (indoor, outdoor_day or
   outdoor_night; goal_a or goal_b; px4 or ardupilot; justice_d; no_sd when there is no SD card).
   To change them: tools/deploy.sh pull, then EVENT_DAY step 0 again.
2. ArduPilot only: write the field's lat, lon, alt into origin_lat_lon_alt in settings.json. Take them from a map
   before the day, not from a GNSS receiver at the field (Stage 1: a GNSS-free start is a bonus route).
3. Copy: NO_RESTART=1 tools/deploy.sh. ArduPilot, after item 2: FORCE=1 NO_RESTART=1 tools/deploy.sh
   (origin_lat_lon_alt counts as a calibration value: without FORCE=1 the copy stops. Rehearsal 2026-11-20.)
4. Do EVENT_DAY step 1 again. The px4 profile adds MAV_0_RATE.
5. Do EVENT_DAY step 2. Write down the fix age.
6. PX4: set EKF2_DELAY_MAX from the fix age (DECISION_CARD row 7). Copy again.
   ArduPilot: VISO_DELAY_MS stays 140 until EVENT_DAY step 7 measures it.
7. Do EVENT_DAY step 3 (mask).
STOP RULE: every param line shows PASS. The fix age is under 150 ms. The heading passes. The mask passes.
If not: use DECISION_CARD rows 7, 8 and 13. Do not go on with a FAIL line.

HOUR 3 (2:00 to 3:00). Calibration and ground tests, propellers off.
1. Do EVENT_DAY steps 4, 5, 6 and 6B.
   The calibration also finds the lens and writes lens_measured into settings.json (Justice D).
2. On the laptop: tools/deploy.sh pull. This gets the calibration, the mask and the new logs from the board's log_dir and logs/.
2b. Lens, Justice D, optional: solve the recording on the laptop.
   Type: ~/.venvs/vio/bin/python apb_main.py calib --rec logs/rec_<stamp>.npz
   On the ground, type: FORCE=1 NO_RESTART=1 tools/deploy.sh
   (Without FORCE=1 the push stops, because the calibration differs. FORCE=1 also skips the stop for a flying aircraft.)
2c. Lens with a checkerboard, development area only: after step 2, type:
   ~/.venvs/vio/bin/python tools/lens_calib.py <folder | video | rec.npz> --settings settings.json
   On the ground, type: FORCE=1 NO_RESTART=1 tools/deploy.sh
   On the board, type: python3 apb_main.py calib. It fits one focal scale against the target lens.
   Then type on the laptop: tools/deploy.sh pull
   A checkerboard is a development aid: fly a hand-turn lens in the scored hold (EVENT_DAY step 4).
3. Keep a copy: cp settings.json good_calib.json
STOP RULE: the two calibrations agree within 3 ms. The focal scale is 0.98 to 1.02 (analog camera, or a target lens). Justice D with no target lens: the "measured lens written to lens_measured" line shows PASS. Every bench value is in its range. Each switch position gives a mode of EVENT_DAY step 6B.
If not: do EVENT_DAY step 4 once more. If it fails again, do not fly. Check the camera mount (DECISION_CARD row 6).

HOUR 4 (3:00 to 4:00). First low hover.
1. Do EVENT_DAY step 7. Its item 1 starts the program with tools/deploy.sh ("apb_main.py run --raw" in the
   background). Its last items pull the logs and set the video delay and VISO_DELAY_MS.
2. Run: ~/.venvs/vio/bin/python tools/plot_log.py logs/<newest run_*.csv>. Look at the PNG.
3. Fly the hover once more with the new values (EVENT_DAY step 7, items 1 to 14; no wiggle).
STOP RULE: the drone stayed above the point. Fixes per second stayed at 10 or more. The fix age stayed under 150 ms.
If not: find the row in DECISION_CARD for what you saw. Make that one change. Fly this hour's hover once more.

HOUR 5 (4:00 to 5:00). Tune at the target height.
1. Fly a 60 s hold at the target height (EVENT_DAY step 7, items 1 to 14). Give small stick inputs, or fly in wind.
   A still hover tunes nothing.
2. Land. Stop the program. On the laptop: tools/deploy.sh pull
3. Run: ~/.venvs/vio/bin/python tools/replay_tune.py "logs/raw_<stamp>_*.npz" (under one minute on this laptop).
   Use its patch only from a recording at 100 m or higher, and only when the timing wiggle (EVENT_DAY step 7,
   item 15) did not pass. Otherwise merge no part of the patch. The delay and readout then come from the wiggle
   (flown at 40 m or higher), or from the ground calibration (EVENT_DAY step 4). Keep the focal scale from calib.
   (Rehearsal 2026-11-20, ArduCopter SITL flight at 100 m, Justice D, delay set 30 ms too long, one flight: the
   wiggle found the true 40 ms; replay_tune on the same flight's chunks found 28 ms, and also with the hold chunks
   only. The board records about 12 pictures each second when Hold takes 80 ms each.)
   (Rehearsal 2026-11-19, analog camera, one sim recording at each height, delay set 30 ms too long. 100 m: patch =
   delay 6 ms from the truth. 30 m: patch = delay 89 ms off, readout 0 (rolling-shutter correction off), focal 8 % off.
   10 m: patch = focal 1.038 (true 1.0) and two Hold settings; the wrong delay stayed. The tool scores by fix
   jitter, and at low height a wrong value gave less jitter. integ/rehearsal_2026-11-19/README.txt)
   (Rehearsal 2026-11-20, Justice D, one sim recording at each height, delay 30 ms too long. 100 m: patch = delay
   1.5 ms from the truth; the error against the truth went back to that of the true settings. 60 m: patch = focal
   8 % off, and the error grew 2.5 times. 30 m: patch = delay 7 ms short and focal 2.7 % off; the error grew.
   10 m: no patch; the wrong delay stayed. integ/rehearsal_2026-11-20/README.txt)
4. If it changed a value (100 m or higher, no wiggle result only): python3 tools/use_profile.py merge logs/tune/tune_patch.json
   Then FORCE=1 tools/deploy.sh (the patch changes calibration values: without FORCE=1 the copy stops).
5. If video_delay_s changed by more than 10 ms: do EVENT_DAY step 4 again (DECISION_CARD row 12).
6. Fly the same 60 s hold again (EVENT_DAY step 7, items 1 to 14). Compare the two plot_log PNGs.
STOP RULE: the second flight is not worse (fixes per second, fix age, drift). Do two tune loops at most.
If it is worse: cp good_calib.json settings.json. Copy it with tools/deploy.sh.

HOUR 6 (5:00 to 6:00). Choose the height.
1. Goal A: hold 30 s at each height step (outdoors 30 m, 60 m, then 100 m if the rules allow it).
2. Goal B: hold 30 s at the lowest height that the rules allow.
3. Indoors: use steps between 2 m and 15 m.
4. Pull and plot each flight.
STOP RULE: choose the highest step (goal A) or the lowest step (goal B) where the drone stayed above the point.
Fixes per second must stay at 10 or more for the full hold. Write the height down.
If no step works: use DECISION_CARD rows 3 and 5.

HOUR 7 (6:00 to 7:00). Timed dress rehearsal.
1. Fly the trial sequence exactly: power, program (tools/deploy.sh), unplug the cable, propellers, 60 s wait (PX4),
   Altitude mode, climb, Position mode, hold, land, propellers off, cable, pull.
2. Time each step. Use only the commands of the trial.
3. Restart the program once on the ground with tools/deploy.sh. The log must show "running: yes".
STOP RULE: the full sequence runs with no laptop work during the flight and fits the trial time.
If not: write down the slow step. Fix only that step.

HOUR 8 (7:00 to 8:00). Freeze.
0. After the last flight of the day: do EVENT_DAY step 8. It puts the aircraft's own flight controller parameters back.
1. Make no change to the code.
2. If the trial is after dark: do EVENT_DAY step 4 in the night light and step 6 (DECISION_CARD row 15).
3. On the laptop: tools/deploy.sh pull. Copy settings.json and the logs folder to a USB stick.
4. Charge the batteries. Check the cables and the propellers.
STOP RULE: python3 tools/use_profile.py diff settings.json good_<last>.json shows only the calibration of step 2.
If a problem remains: copy the last good settings file to settings.json. Fly that.
