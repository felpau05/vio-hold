PILOT SAFETY: TEAM POSITION-HOLD PROGRAM (ArduCopter, event day 2026-11-21)
Labels: (source) = read in the ArduPilot code. (sim) = simulator test. (not tested) = never tried.
Nothing ran on this aircraft yet. You decide if it flies. Evidence: integ/pilot_safety/README.txt.

TO TAKE OVER: move the mode switch to AltHold, Stabilize or Acro. (source)
The FC accepts these modes at any time in flight. (source)
Do not use Return to Home to take over. With GPS off, it flies on our position, or the FC refuses it. (source)
The FC does not detect a slow, wrong drift of our position. Watch the aircraft in Loiter. (source, sim)

1. WHAT OUR PROGRAM CAN AND CANNOT DO
- It sends a position about 10 times each second. In Loiter, this is the only position the FC has. (source)
- It never arms the aircraft. (source)
- With our event settings, it never changes the flight mode. (source)
- It never sends a flight target. It never moves your sticks. (source)
- It sets Home to a field point in its settings. RTL flies to that point. (source)
- With the outdoor profile, it sends no position below 3 m. (source)
- With the crew's agreement, our checklist writes FC parameters on the ground. It turns the GPS off for the autopilot.
- We save the aircraft's own values first. We write them back after our last test (checklist step 1 item 2b, step 8).
- With a gnss_setup or gnss_fallback profile, the program also tells the FC which position source to use. (sim)
- With these profiles, Position Hold also starts when our program is not ready. The FC then holds on GNSS. (sim)
- With gnss_fallback, the FC holds on GNSS after 3 s with no fix from us. (sim)

2. HOW TO TAKE OVER
- Move the switch to AltHold, Stabilize or Acro. These modes do not use our position. (source)
- AltHold keeps the height but not the position. Fly against the wind at once. (sim)
- Sim, sticks centred, wind 20 km/h with gusts of 40 km/h: AltHold drifted 36 m in 10 s, up to 6.5 m/s. (sim)
- If Loiter does not start, the FC stays in the old mode. It shows "Mode change to LOITER failed". (source)
- To try Loiter again, move the switch out of Position Hold and back. (source)

3. FAILURES: WHAT THE AIRCRAFT DOES, WHAT YOU DO
- Slow wrong position: the aircraft moves away in Loiter with no warning. Select AltHold. Fly it back. (source, sim)
- Sim: a 1 m/s error gave no FC text. A takeover 3 s after the first 3 m came at 7 to 8 m. (sim)
- Our position stops (program stop, camera covered): 7 to 8 s later the FC shows "EKF variance" and
  "EKF Failsafe" and changes to Land. With FS_EKF_ACTION 2 it changes to AltHold. (sim: 7.2 s and 7.9 s)
- Correction: in the sim the aircraft stayed within 2 m until the failsafe. Our first page said it drifts. (sim)
- Do not wait for the failsafe. A real sensor can drift more. Select AltHold when the aircraft moves. (source)
- In Land, the aircraft waits 4 s, then descends and drifts with the wind. Select AltHold, Stabilize or Acro. (source)
- After this failsafe, Loiter does not engage. Fly manually and land. (source)
- Position jump: the FC refuses it at once and shows "EKF3 lane switch". The aircraft does not move. (sim)
- About 7 s later the FC can accept the jump, still without a move. Home and RTL then move by the jump. (sim)
- Radio loss: by default the FC starts RTL in every mode, also in Acro. RTL then flies on our position. (source)
- We ask the crew to set FS_THR_ENABLE 3 (Land). Check that value. (source)
- A battery failsafe set to RTL also flies on our position. We ask for Land or a warning. (source)
- One board holds the FC and our computer. A board power loss stops the FC. (source: Orqa documents)
- We do not know if a computer restart stops the FC. Bench step B7 checks this. (not tested)
- A wrong position bends the attitude a little: up to 2.4 deg of tilt and 12 deg of heading error. (sim)
- AltHold and Stabilize still held height and attitude after the takeover. Acro was not flown in the sim. (sim)
- If the aircraft does not answer the sticks normally after the switch, land at once. (not tested)

4. ARMING, AND WHAT WE ASK YOU TO CHECK ON THE BENCH (propellers off, 10 minutes)
- With the vision arming check on, the FC refuses to arm. You see "PreArm: VisOdom: not healthy". (sim)
- The crew can turn off only that check: ARMING_CHECK 1835006 (ArduPilot 4.6). (sim)
- On ArduPilot 4.7 and later, the same change is ARMING_SKIPCHK 262144. (source, not tested)
- With that change, the simulated aircraft armed, climbed to 10 m and held with no warning. (sim)
- Do not let anyone set ARMING_CHECK 0 or ARMING_SKIPCHK -1. That turns off every arming check. (source)
- B0: the crew shows FS_EKF_ACTION, FS_THR_ENABLE, BATT_FS_*_ACT, FS_DR_ENABLE and the arming checks. (not tested)
- B1: each switch position shows the mode name you expect. No position gives RTL. (= checklist step 6B) (sim)
- B2: our program is off. In Position Hold the aircraft must not arm. (sim: "VisOdom: not healthy")
- B3: we freeze our program. The FC must show "EKF variance" within 15 s. The mode stays. (source)
- B4: we cover the camera for 15 s. The FC must show "EKF variance" again. (source)
- B5: we kill our program. It must not restart. B6: each switch move changes the mode within 1 s. (source)
- B7: we restart our computer. The FC must keep running. (not tested)
- First low flight, 2 m, AltHold: select Position Hold. The FC must refuse it (not with a gnss profile). (source)
- First low flight, at 10 m: Loiter for 10 s, then AltHold, then Acro. The aircraft must answer at once. (not tested)

5. WHAT IS NOT TESTED
- Sim only: Loiter to AltHold and Stabilize, a program stop, a slow wrong position and a jump, at 10 m and 30 m.
- One simulator run per case: one site, one wind, ideal sensors. Acro was not flown. (sim)
- No test of a radio failsafe or of RTL.
- PX4: Position mode changes to Altitude mode when our position stops. (source) (sim) Other PX4 paths: not tested.
