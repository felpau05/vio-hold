DECISION CARD: what you see, the most likely cause, what to change
Read the log first: ~/.venvs/vio/bin/python tools/plot_log.py logs/run_*.csv (one PNG in about 1 s).
After each change: run tools/deploy.sh, then python3 apb_main.py check on the board.
PX4 names are on the left of "/", ArduCopter 4.6 names are on the right.
Safety first: if the drone moves away, select Altitude mode (AltHold) at once.

1  SEE    The drone swings or flies away right after Position mode (Loiter) starts.
   CAUSE  The picture is turned or mirrored (FINDINGS G1, H3). Or att_source is not "gyro" (fly-away).
   DO     Select Altitude mode. Land. Run python3 apb_main.py calib (EVENT_DAY step 4). Do the bench test (step 6).
          Run python3 tools/use_profile.py show. att_source must not appear in the list of changed values.
2  SEE    The drone flies circles around the hold point ("toilet bowl").
   CAUSE  The picture is turned 90 deg (G1). The PX4 VIO docs name the camera direction (papers_control.md S13).
   DO     Select Altitude mode. Land. Run calib (EVENT_DAY step 4). Do the bench test (step 6).
3  SEE    Positions stop over plain ground: grass, snow, a plain floor.
   CAUSE  Too few points (Hold MIN_PTS 20), or the capture check refuses a flat picture (no_signal_mad). Digital camera: it refuses a flat picture with a median under 64 grey (BLACK_GREY), also with OSD text.
   DO     Read the reasons panel in plot_log. Ask the pilot for a test position over textured ground.
          Do not put tarps, lamps or marks on the ground. The rules do not permit them in a scored flight.
          Do not lower hold_cfg min_pts. No test shows that it is safe (E1).
4  SEE    Positions stop in a still hover ("waiting for image motion that proves the picture is live").
   CAUSE  The liveness check needs picture motion (hold_cfg move_px 1.5 px, live_s 1.0 s).
   DO     Wait 3 s (the sim accepted a still start after 2.7 s). If it stays, give a small roll input.
5  SEE    The hold is good low but poor high.
   CAUSE  Every tilt, timing and lens error grows with the height (H1, G2, E5). Calm air: the autopilot's tilt error.
   DO     Record 60 s high (100 m or higher) with --raw. Run ~/.venvs/vio/bin/python tools/replay_tune.py logs/raw_*.npz.
          Below 100 m, merge no replay_tune patch: at 30 m it set a wrong delay, readout and focal together (EIGHT_HOURS hour 5).
          Fly lower for goal B.
6  SEE    The picture is mirrored or turned (nose down does not move the picture toward its top edge).
   CAUSE  top and mirror in settings.json are wrong for this camera mount.
   DO     Run python3 apb_main.py calib. Do not edit top or mirror by hand. Do the bench test (step 6).
7  SEE    The fix age is 150 ms or more (check line, or the ms panel in plot_log).
   CAUSE  Video delay plus processing time. The governor "powersave" slows the CPU (board_check.sh).
   DO     PX4: put EKF2_DELAY_MAX = fix age + 100 in params_px4 (EVENT_DAY step 2). Run check --set-params. Reboot.
          ArduPilot: set VISO_DELAY_MS in params_ardupilot to the fix age (0 to 250).
8  SEE    The autopilot refuses Position mode (Loiter).
   CAUSE  No fixes yet, or a parameter or the origin is missing (H2, EVENT_DAY step 7).
   DO     Run python3 apb_main.py check. Every param line must show PASS. Fix FAIL with check --set-params.
          ArduPilot: set origin_lat_lon_alt in settings.json (the field's lat, lon, alt). PX4: wait 60 s after run.
          Climb above min_height_m first (indoor profile: 1 m, outdoor: 3 m). The program sends nothing lower.
9  SEE    Steady drift in one direction.
   CAUSE  Fixes stopped and the wind carries the drone (see 3 and 4). Or a yaw turn after the start (E5, H8).
   DO     Read fixes per second in plot_log. Do not yaw during the hold. Run calib again to measure tilt_deg.
          Only the camera view sits off the target in wind? That is the lean into the wind, not drift.
          Wind and a slow return: raise MPC_XY_VEL_I_ACC (PX4, 0.4). PSC_VELXY_I is at its 4.6 maximum (1.0).
          Test a gain change in SITL first.
10 SEE    The hold wobbles back and forth.
   CAUSE  The delay that the autopilot uses differs from the fix age (px4_vision.md section 3), or the gain is high.
   DO     Set EKF2_EV_DELAY / VISO_DELAY_MS to the fix age. Then lower MPC_XY_P (0.95) / PSC_POSXY_P (1.0).
          Test a gain change in SITL first (papers_control.md S12).
11 SEE    Fewer than 10 fixes per second (plot_log panel 3, line min_send_hz).
   CAUSE  The CPU is too slow, or frames are refused (reasons panel).
   DO     Set the governor to performance (board_check.sh prints the command). Try hold_cfg fb_every 3 (H6).
12 SEE    Many frames refused with "image motion does not match the attitude".
   CAUSE  Wrong video delay, readout, lens scale or camera direction.
   DO     Run replay_tune on the last recording. In the sim it found 40 ms delay errors and a wrong readout sign.
          It can miss a delay that is 20 ms too short. Run calib again if it changes video_delay_s by over 10 ms.
13 SEE    "attitude late", "attitude stale" or ATTITUDE under 100 Hz in check.
   CAUSE  Other streams fill the link (H5).
   DO     PX4: add px4 to the apply line (MAV_0_RATE 23040). Run check --set-params. Stop ULog streaming.
          ArduPilot: set the SRn_ rates of the APB port to 0 (EVENT_DAY step 7).
14 SEE    Indoors: frames refused with "height ... m under 15 m".
   CAUSE  The outdoor minimum height is active.
   DO     python3 tools/use_profile.py apply indoor goal_b <px4 or ardupilot>. Then tools/deploy.sh.
15 SEE    The trial is after dark, and the calibration was made in daylight.
   CAUSE  The exposure is longer at night. It moves the video delay (H11, +8 ms in the sim).
   DO     Run calib in the night light (EVENT_DAY step 4). Apply outdoor_night. Do the bench test (step 6).

Rows 16 to 24: Justice D (digital camera) messages. PX4/ArduPilot names do not apply. Text in quotes comes from the code.
16 SEE    check: "video: cannot open the camera at <video_device or video_pipeline>".
   CAUSE  OpenCV cannot open the camera: the cable, video_device, or the V4L2 backend.
   DO     Check the camera cable and video_device (CSI 1 is /dev/video2). Run sh tools/board_check.sh.
          Set video_pipeline in settings.json. If that fails, set video_command (EVENT_DAY step 2).
17 SEE    check: "video rate <n> fps (setting video_fps 60)" shows FAIL.
   CAUSE  The camera rate is not within 10 % of video_fps.
   DO     Set video_fps to the camera's rate. With video_command, check video_command and pixel_format.
18 SEE    "cannot read pixel format '<x>' as grey" or "cannot cut the video_command output into frames".
   CAUSE  pixel_format is not a format that the program reads.
   DO     Set pixel_format to YUYV, UYVY, NV12 or GREY. Use the format that board_check.sh lists for /dev/video2.
19 SEE    check: "video_command stopped with exit code <n>" or "video_command gave no frame in 2 s".
   CAUSE  The command fails, or it does not write raw frames of video_size to its standard output.
   DO     Read the error above the line. Check video_command and pixel_format in settings.json.
20 SEE    check: "lens profile '<name>' is <w>x<h>, the picture <w>x<h>" shows FAIL.
   CAUSE  The lens profile and the picture have different shapes.
   DO     Set lens_name to a 16:9 profile. Or run python3 tools/use_profile.py apply <profile names> justice_d.
21 SEE    calib: "FAIL k1 not found" or "FAIL calibration".
   CAUSE  The drone turned too little, or the tracked points did not reach far enough from the optical axis.
   DO     Read the reason in the line. Turn the drone more about all three axes. Run calib again (EVENT_DAY step 4).
22 SEE    run refuses to start: "capture is '<x>' but the calibration was made with '<y>'. Run calib again."
   CAUSE  The camera or the capture settings changed after the calibration.
   DO     Run python3 apb_main.py calib (EVENT_DAY step 4). Start run again.
23 SEE    check: "free disk <n> GB at <path>" shows FAIL.
   CAUSE  A raw recording takes about 14 MB each second at 60 Hz. The 4 GB eMMC is too small.
   DO     Set log_dir to /sdcard/logs in settings.json. Or delete old logs.
24 SEE    board_check.sh: "board temperature <n> C: the board stops at 95 C" shows FAIL.
   CAUSE  The board is hot. It stops at 95 C.
   DO     Cool the board before the flight. Run sh tools/board_check.sh again.

Rows 25 to 33: more Justice D messages. Text in quotes comes from the code.
25 SEE    check: "frame stamps from <x>: intervals differ from their median by <n> ms (p95, limit 12 ms for a hold at 40 m)" shows FAIL.
   CAUSE  The frame stamps jitter, or the camera lost frames. A stamp error moves the fix.
   DO     Use driver stamps: video_device, no video_command. Stop other programs on the board. Run check again.
26 SEE    check: "frame stamps from <x>: <n> intervals in 2 s, too few to measure (need 10)".
   CAUSE  The camera gave too few frames in 2 s.
   DO     Make the camera give frames (rows 16 to 19). Run check again.
27 SEE    run: "FAIL no camera frame for 2 s: the camera stopped. The program opens it again."
   CAUSE  The camera gave no frame for 2 s. The program closes the camera and opens it again.
   DO     If this repeats, check the camera cable, and video_device or video_command in settings.json.
28 SEE    run: "the raw recorder stopped: the disk at <path> has <n> GB free, less than min_free_gb <n>. The hold continues."
   CAUSE  The free space is under min_free_gb. The raw recording stops. The hold goes on.
   DO     Delete old logs before the next flight. Check that log_dir is /sdcard/logs (row 23).
29 SEE    run: "log write failed: <error>. The hold continues without a log."
   CAUSE  The log file cannot be written. The fixes go on.
   DO     Free disk space at the log folder before the next flight.
30 SEE    calib: "FAIL calibration: the picture is <w>x<h>, but lens profile '<name>' is <w>x<h>."
   CAUSE  The lens profile and the picture have different shapes.
   DO     Set lens_name to a Justice D profile. Run calib again.
31 SEE    calib: "FAIL calibration: the lens fit ended at the edge of its search range".
   CAUSE  The lens is outside the search range, or the recording is poor.
   DO     Record again. If it fails again, measure the lens with a checkerboard (EVENT_DAY, laptop procedure).
32 SEE    calib: "FAIL focal scale is outside 0.98-1.02: the lens measured with a target does not fit this recording."
   CAUSE  lens_measured (target) does not match this camera or this picture.
   DO     Measure the lens again with tools/lens_calib.py --settings. Or set lens_measured to null. Then run calib.
33 SEE    check or run: "lens_measured (<by>) is for <w>x<h> pictures, but the picture is <w>x<h>."
   CAUSE  lens_measured is from a picture of another shape.
   DO     Set lens_measured to null in settings.json. Run calib. Or measure the lens again.

Rows 34 to 38: the pilot's switch, the light and the recovery demonstration. Text in quotes comes from the code.
34 SEE    The drone jumps or swings when the pilot moves the switch to Position mode.
   CAUSE  The fixes stopped near the switch, or the hold point moved at the switch.
   DO     Find the note "engaged: FC mode ..." in the run log. Read the rows near it: sent, why and the mode column.
          Do not use the fresh_kf profile until apsim/engage_results.txt shows that it helps.
35 SEE    At dusk or at night the run log shows "black or flat picture (lens covered?)" while the lens is clear.
   CAUSE  The ground texture is very faint. The picture check refuses a picture with a texture spread under
          no_signal_mad (2 grey at 1/8 size). Hold could use some of these pictures (a synthetic test only).
   DO     Fly over ground with more contrast, or fly lower. Do not lower no_signal_mad: a covered lens with
          OSD text then gives false fixes (tests_apb/test_robust.py D7).
36 SEE    The hold gets worse when the light changes (sun to cloud, dusk, the camera's switch to black and white).
   CAUSE  A keyframe taken while the camera's exposure moves matches later frames worse (assumed, not measured).
   DO     Add the kf_bright profile (EVENT_DAY, optional new hold options). Fly the same test again. Compare the logs.
37 SEE    The run log shows "recovery test: video dropped" in a flight that is not the recovery demonstration.
   CAUSE  The recovery_demo profile is still on, or the file outage_now is in log_dir.
   DO     Apply your profile names again without recovery_demo. Copy the settings (deploy.sh). Delete outage_now.
38 SEE    At 5 m: many "re-anchored" notes and "whole picture: halves disagree", gaps of several seconds without a fix.
   CAUSE  The whole-picture path fails its halves check (limit 4 px). ArduCopter SITL at 5 m: up to 16 re-anchors per
          minute, gaps up to 6.3 s (apsim/low_ap_results.txt). In SITL, Hold ran almost only on that path at 5-10 m
          (48 points = its 8 x 6 grid; halves gap median 4.3-5.1 px): the sim photo has almost no corners at that
          height. Real ground has more texture, but grass that moves in the propeller wash can also cause it.
   DO     Fly the hold at 10 m or higher when the level allows it. Check fixes per second in plot_log.
