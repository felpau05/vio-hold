REVIEW OF THE PATCH (step 2)
a. Cause or sign: the cause. flow_payload sends whole counts (int(round(x))), so each message lost its fraction and
   any speed under 0.5 count per message (0.25 m/s at 5 m and 20 Hz) became 0. Carrying the remainder keeps the sum
   of the sent counts within one count of the true sum.
b. Flight loop: msp_link.MspLink.send_speed, which run_live calls (fc.send_speed) only when the Betaflight option is
   on. With the option off (the event setup) no flight-loop line changes: fc_link.connect never makes an MspLink.
c. Input the author did not test: a gap over GAP_S (0.5 s) clears the remainder (right). A refused send (budget, no
   fix) keeps it (right). A yaw turn applies the old remainder (under 0.5 count) in the new body axes (small). A
   non-finite count (roll at 90 degrees: cos = 0) makes flow_payload raise with or without M1; mode_run then opens a
   new link, which also clears the remainder. Not reachable in a hold.
