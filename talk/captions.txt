hold_vs_height.png
Caption: RMS distance from the hold point over a 60 s hold, against height. Circles: PX4 SITL with our fixes. Squares: ArduCopter SITL with our fixes. Open diamonds: a perfect position (the true position sent as the fix), PX4 and ArduCopter drawn slightly left and right of each height. Justice D camera model, wind 20 gusting 40 km/h. Each mark is one run. Different heights come from different run sets, so compare points at the same height only.
Source files: px4sim/low_outage_results.txt (PX4 at 5 and 10 m; perfect position at 5 and 10 m), apsim/low_ap_results.txt (ArduCopter at 5 and 10 m; perfect position at 5 and 10 m), integ/justice_d/summary.txt (ArduCopter closed-loop rows jd_apb_50m_w20g40_s11 and jd_apb_100m_w20g40_s11 to s13). Runs with a video outage and calm runs are left out.
Runs: PX4 4 (5 m: 2, 10 m: 2). ArduCopter 8 (5 m: 2, 10 m: 2, 50 m: 1, 100 m: 3). Perfect position 4 (one per autopilot and height). Seeds: PX4 1, 2; ArduCopter 1, 2 at 5 and 10 m, 11 at 50 m, 11 to 13 at 100 m; perfect position seed 1. There is no PX4 run at 50 or 100 m and no run above 100 m.

betaflight_modes.png

Source files: betasim/results/closed_g0_s1.json, closed_g0_s2.json, closed_g0.2_s1.json, closed_g0.2_s2.json (field rms_m); summary in betasim/results/matrix.txt.
Runs: 4 (2 modes x 2 seeds). Seeds: 1, 2.
