HOW WE GOT HERE

One morning on the aircraft

Before

No aircraft yet, so we built simulators of the camera, the flight controller and the ground, and tested the hold in them.

10:30

Live camera and motion sensors on the board, and a clock bug in the sensor driver caught and fixed: it would have broken the rolling-shutter correction.

11:00

Live position on a web page served by the drone itself, ready for the judge’s bench test.

11:40

Caught the datasheet out: trusting its lens would have made every distance 14 % short. We calibrated our own.

13:00

First walk failed: 1 m read as 0.46 m. Within the hour the data told us why, and the fix was under way.

Times are when each result was committed to our repository on 21 Nov 2026

This is the entrepreneurial-drive slide: speed, ownership, and learning from failure. Tell it as a story, fast: we did not wait for the hardware; when it came we had it streaming within an hour; we did not trust the paperwork; and when the first test failed we found out why the same hour.
