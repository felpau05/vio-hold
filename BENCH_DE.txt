BENCH ITEMS D, E1, E2: REHEARSAL ON THE REAL AIRCRAFT, PROPELLERS OFF

Source: Stage 2, section 05, steps 6, 7 and 8. Section 04: we can fly only after we bank D and E.
Only a result on the real aircraft counts. A simulator or a unit test is not a rehearsal result.
Order of work: section 1 (questions), 4 (copy and freeze), 5 (start), 6 (R0), 7 (D), 8 (E1), 9 (E2).


0. WHAT THE JUDGE DOES (Stage 2)

D (15 pts). The judge turns the engage switch on and off. The judge does this once with our solution
ready and once with it not ready.
  Pass: Ready, switch on: our commands appear. Switch off: pilot control at once.
  Pass: Not ready: Betaflight receives roll, pitch and yaw 1500, never minimum values.
E1 (8 pts). The judge covers the Justice D for 3 s.
  Pass: Within 1 s our solution shows not ready. Roll, pitch and yaw stay at 1500 while covered.
  Pass: Normal output comes only after the camera is uncovered.
E2 (7 pts). The judge power-cycles the APB.
  Pass: Our solution restarts by itself and shows ready within 60 s.
  Pass: Betaflight gets safe values from our first command.
Session: 10 minutes, 2 tries for each item, 3 sessions or fewer. Declare the items at the start.
Set-up: propellers off, battery in, radio on. The judge holds the aircraft level, about 1 m above the floor.
The judge reads the Betaflight Receiver tab on the organizer laptop, on the FC USB.
At the first session, the judge also asks for the GNSS answer (section 12, step 0).

2. EQUIPMENT

- The aircraft, propellers off, a charged battery.
- The bound radio. Find the engage switch (TL2, CH10) and the arm switch (CH5).
- Laptop 1 on the APB USB-C. Open our page: http://192.168.75.1:8080
- Laptop 2 (or laptop 1) on the FC USB. Open Betaflight Configurator, Receiver tab. This is the judge's view.
- A phone that records video, 60 fps if possible. Use it to count frames for the 1 s limit of E1.
- A stopwatch for E2.
- A dark card, about 10 x 10 cm, white paper, a dark cloth. Use them for "not ready" in D and for E1.
- A dry lens cloth.
- A floor like the judging floor. Look at the judging floor before the rehearsal.


3. SAFETY RULES FOR EACH REHEARSAL

1. Remove all propellers. Examine each motor.
2. Do not arm. Keep the arm switch (CH5) low for the full rehearsal.
3. Keep the engage switch low when you connect the battery. Exception: E2, section 9.
   (Stage 2 section 03: before our first command, the FC reads minimum on four channels while the switch is on.)
4. Do not stop our program to show "not ready". The FC has no timeout. It keeps the last command.
5. In Betaflight Configurator, change nothing. Do not push Save. Only read the Receiver tab.
6. Do not use tools/deploy.sh. It is the Stage 1 tool. It starts apb_main.py, not apb_live.py.
7. Do not touch the Justice D lens. Skin oil makes the picture soft. Then READY can fail to come.
   If somebody touches the lens, clean it with the dry lens cloth.
8. Before each power cut of the APB, run: ssh root@192.168.75.1 sync
   (A file that is not on the disk yet can be empty after a power cut.)


4. COPY AND FREEZE THE PROGRAM. DO THIS BEFORE R0.

The program changes often. A pass counts only for the code that the board ran.
Run these commands on laptop 1, in the vio-hold-stage2-bench folder.
1. Copy the program files together. An older apb_live.py does not know --rc. Then the program stops at start.
   Do not copy settings.json. The board keeps its own copy.
     scp apb_live.py apb_lift.py msp_rc.py msp_link.py apb_est.py apb_main.py apb_sensors.py calib.py \
         capture.py fc_link.py hold.py settings.py v4l2_mmap.py root@192.168.75.1:/data/vio/
     scp tools/apb_ctl.sh tools/vio_boot.sh tools/vio-live.service root@192.168.75.1:/data/vio/
     ssh root@192.168.75.1 sync
2. Run: ssh root@192.168.75.1 sh /data/vio/vio_boot.sh check
   Pass: the last line shows "PASS files and start arguments".
   Pass: the md5 values are the same as on the laptop: md5sum apb_live.py apb_lift.py msp_rc.py
3. Write the three md5 values in the record sheet (section 10).
4. Do not change these files until the judged session. If a value changes, start the count of passes again.


5. START THE PROGRAM

Before you install the boot unit (from laptop 1):
  ssh root@192.168.75.1 sh /data/vio/apb_ctl.sh start --height 1.0 --pos floor --rc
  (Use the same arguments as /data/vio/boot.env, if that file exists.)
After you install the boot unit, the program starts at power-up. To see its state:
  ssh root@192.168.75.1 sh /data/vio/vio_boot.sh status
Check: the page shows "FC out roll ... engage switch off".
If the page shows "(no FC reply)", stop. The MSP link to the FC does not work. See section 11.
Write down the laptop time and the board time (ssh root@192.168.75.1 date). The board clock is wrong.
You need the two times to find the logs.


6. R0: CHANNEL ORDER AND ENGAGE SWITCH. DO THIS ONCE BEFORE D.

Why: Stage 2 gives the order roll, pitch, throttle, yaw. A wrong order puts our throttle on another channel.
1. Stop the program: ssh root@192.168.75.1 sh /data/vio/apb_ctl.sh stop
   If you installed the boot unit, run this instead: ssh root@192.168.75.1 systemctl stop vio-live
   Start the program by hand (section 5) with one more argument: --rc-hover 1350
   Do not put --rc-hover 1350 in boot.env. It can stay there by mistake.
2. Hold the aircraft still. Cover the lens with the card. Wait until the page shows NOT READY.
3. Put the engage switch high.
4. Read the four channels on the Receiver tab.
   Pass: Roll 1500, Pitch 1500, Yaw 1500, Throttle 1350.
   If 1350 is on a different channel, the order is wrong. Stop. Tell the owner of msp_rc.py. Do not do D.
   If no channel shows 1350, MSP OVERRIDE is not on. See section 11.
5. Read AUX 6 (CH10) on the Receiver tab. Do this with the switch low, then with the switch high.
   Pass: high is 1800 or more. Low is less than 1800. (The FC engages MSP OVERRIDE only at 1800 to 2100.)
   If high is less than 1800, the FC does not engage. Tell the organizer. Do not change the FC.
   Pass: the page shows "engage switch ON" and "engage switch off" at the same times.
6. Put the switch low. Remove the card. Stop the program: ssh root@192.168.75.1 sh /data/vio/apb_ctl.sh stop
   Start it again without --rc-hover 1350 (section 5). If you installed the boot unit: vio_boot.sh restart.


7. D REHEARSAL (Stage 2 step 6)

One person holds the aircraft level, about 1 m above the floor. A second person uses the radio.
The phone films the Receiver tab and our page.
D, ready:
1. Wait until the page shows READY.
2. Put the engage switch high.
   Pass: roll and pitch stay near 1500 at the switch-on. They do not jump to about 1400 or 1600.
   (Before this step, do not push "Zero here". Carry the aircraft 1 m first. msp_rc.py 8b40e23f and later
   send neutral until the zero at the switch-on. Older code can jump for 0.05 to 0.25 s.
   If you see a jump, write it down and tell the owners of msp_rc.py and apb_live.py.)
3. Carry the aircraft 0.5 m to the right. Then carry it 0.5 m forward.
   Pass: the page shows "engage switch ON".
   Pass: Roll goes below 1500 after the move to the right (about 1450 at gain 100 us/m).
   Pass: Pitch goes below 1500 after the move forward. Yaw stays 1500. Throttle shows the hover value.
4. Put the engage switch low. Move the radio roll stick.
   Pass: at once, the Receiver tab follows the radio sticks.
D, not ready:
5. Cover the lens with the card. Do not touch the lens. Wait until the page shows NOT READY.
6. Put the engage switch high. Carry the aircraft 0.5 m to the right.
   Pass: Roll, pitch and yaw stay at 1500 for the full time.
   Pass: Throttle shows the hover value. No channel goes to its minimum value.
7. Put the engage switch low. Remove the card.
Do D three times. Record each try in section 10.


8. E1 REHEARSAL (Stage 2 step 7)

1. Hold the aircraft level, about 1 m above the floor. Wait until the page shows READY.
2. Put the engage switch high.
   Pass: the page shows "engage switch ON". The Receiver tab shows the hover value on Throttle.
3. Carry the aircraft 0.5 m to the right. Keep it there.
   Pass: Roll is about 1450. (Then a change to 1500 during the cover is easy to see.)
4. Start the phone video. Film the lens, our page and the Receiver tab together, if possible.
5. Cover the full lens for 3 s. Then remove the cover. Keep the aircraft still during the cover.
   (The floor track does not measure a move while the cover is on the lens. This is not part of E1.)
   Pass: the page shows NOT READY within 1 s after the cover. Count the video frames. Our target is 0.5 s.
   Pass: Roll, pitch and yaw are exactly 1500 on the Receiver tab during the cover. 1499 or 1501 is a fail.
   Pass: after you remove the cover, the page shows READY again, and roll goes back to about 1450.
   Write down how long this takes.
6. Do steps 1 to 5 with each cover. Write down the "why" text on the page for each cover.
   - a palm flat on the lens (no lens contact: keep a fingertip width away)
   - a palm 2 cm, 10 cm and 20 cm from the lens
   - a hand that comes in from the side
   - a fingertip 1 cm from the lens (no contact)
   - the dark card flat at 2 mm, and the dark card 1 cm from the lens
   - white paper, and a dark cloth
7. Cover half of the lens for 3 s. Write down what the page shows.
   If a cover or the half cover keeps READY, ask the judge to use the cover that passed (section 12, step 5).
   Tell the owners of apb_live.py and apb_lift.py which cover kept READY.
8. Put the engage switch low.


9. E2 REHEARSAL (Stage 2 step 8). THIS NEEDS A YES TO Q2.

Install the boot unit once, after section 4.
1. Run: ssh root@192.168.75.1 sh /data/vio/apb_ctl.sh stop
2. Run: ssh root@192.168.75.1 sh /data/vio/vio_boot.sh install
3. Run: ssh root@192.168.75.1 sh /data/vio/vio_boot.sh restart
   Pass: "unit: enabled, active" and "running".
After each new copy of the program, do section 4, then steps 1 and 3 again.
To remove the unit: ssh root@192.168.75.1 sh /data/vio/vio_boot.sh remove
Restart, switch high (the main test, because the judge can see our first command only with the switch high):
4. Propellers off, arm switch low. One person holds the aircraft level by hand, 1 m above the floor,
   as the judge does. Do not use a table.
5. Wait for READY. Put the engage switch high. Carry the aircraft 0.5 m to the right. Roll is about 1450.
6. Start the phone video on the Receiver tab. Run: ssh root@192.168.75.1 sync
7. Cut the APB power with the method of the judge (Q3). Start the stopwatch at the cut.
   Write down how long the power stays off.
8. Do not reload our page. It connects again by itself.
   Pass: during the cut, the page shows NO CONNECTION and "-" in each value (apb_live.py 9787a6f8 and later).
   With older code, the text READY can stay on a red banner. That is not READY.
9. Stop the stopwatch when the banner is green and shows READY, and the "up" value on the info line is small.
   (A small "up" value shows a new program.)
   Pass: READY in 60 s or less from the cut. Our target is 45 s or less.
   Write down the time from the power-on to the end of the text "hold the drone still (gyro bias)".
10. Pass, if the FC stays on: roll stays at about 1450 during the cut (the FC keeps the last command).
    Then roll goes directly to 1500 at our first command. No other value comes between.
    Pass, if the FC restarts too: the four channels go from the start values of the FC directly to
    roll, pitch, yaw 1500 and the hover throttle. No other value comes between.
11. Put the engage switch low.
12. Run: ssh root@192.168.75.1 sh /data/vio/vio_boot.sh status
    Pass: "unit: enabled, active" and "running".
13. Do steps 4 to 12 three times.
Restart, switch low:
14. Do steps 4 to 12 again, with the engage switch low the full time. Skip step 10. Do this three times.
15. If the page shows "no connection to the APB" for more than 30 s, examine the USB network of laptop 1.
    Write down what you did. On the judging day, the page must connect again with no help.

12. AT THE JUDGED SESSION

0. First session only: give the GNSS answer (Stage 2 section 04). Show these three items.
   - The top of apb_live.py: "Inputs are the APB camera, IMU and barometer only: no FC, no GNSS."
   - msp_rc.py, the line ALLOWED = (MSP_SET_RAW_RC, MSP_RC). The program sends only these two MSP commands.
     It reads only the RC channels from the FC, for the engage switch.
   - Say: "Our program uses no GNSS at any time. Thus nothing changes at engage.
     GPS stays connected for your drift measurement. Nothing in apb_live.py calls the GPS functions in msp_link.py."
1. Declare D, E1 or E2 only after it passed three times in a row in the rehearsal, with the same md5 values.
   Before you book: hold the aircraft 1 m above the judging floor, with no cover, in the judging light.
   Pass: the page shows READY. It does not show "lens covered or blurred".
   If it shows "lens covered or blurred", the floor is too plain or the light is too low.
   Write down the "detail" value from the page. Tell the owner of apb_live.py.
   (Real carpet gave detail 15.6. The limit is COVER_FINE 3.0.)
2. Before the session: compare the md5 values on the board with the values on the passing lines.
   Examine the Justice D lens. Clean it with the dry lens cloth.
3. Say to the judge: "After switch on, our zero is here. Please move the aircraft about 0.5 m.
   Then you see our roll and pitch change on the Receiver tab."
4. For D, not ready: "We cover the lens with this card."
5. For E1: "Please put the engage switch on. Wait for READY on our page. Move the aircraft about 0.5 m.
   Then put your palm or this card flat on the lens for 3 s."
6. For E2: "The switch can be on or off. We rehearsed both." Do E2 last. It restarts the APB.
   If the judge touched the lens in E1, ask to clean it before E2.
7. Do not stop our program during the session.
