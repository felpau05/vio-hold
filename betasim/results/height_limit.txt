Height above 10 m: source check (orqa-betaflight 2026.6, commit 09ea1c692f; paths under src/main/). No SITL run:
the check stopped at step 3e, as the task says, because the rangefinder enters the height estimate and no
setting removes it while the flow stays in use.

a. Where the 1000 cm limit applies
   - cli/settings.c 2005: rangefinder_max_range_cm, allowed 50-1000 (default 400, flight/position.c 105).
   - cli/settings.c 1186: poshold_opticalflow_max_range, allowed 50-1000 (default 400, pg/pos_hold_multirotor.c 41).
   - flight/position_estimator.c 100-111: one helper refuses a height under 10 cm or over the limit.
     Used at 468 (height fusion, limit rangefinder_max_range_cm) and 517-525 (flow, limit = the smaller of the two).
   - Not a limit: the MTF02P table value 600 cm (drivers/rangefinder/rangefinder_lidarmt.c 60) is copied to
     rangefinderMaxRangeCm (sensors/rangefinder.c 202-204) and nothing reads it. The driver passes any distance.

   Does the rangefinder enter the height estimate? Yes. feedRangefinderMeasurements (position_estimator.c
   458-492) fuses (rangefinder - its first value after arming) into the vertical Kalman filter with R = 100 cm^2
   (25 cm^2 with RANGEFINDER_PREFER or _ONLY). The barometer gets R = 1500 / (altitude_prefer_baro / 100)
   cm^2, so 1500 at best (445-448). The rangefinder is 15 times stronger. While it is fused, the barometer offset
   is also pulled toward the estimate (cross-calibration, 165, 569-587).
   Which setting removes it: altitude_source = BARO_ONLY or GPS_ONLY (461-465). The same two values also stop
   the flow (512-515). rangefinder_max_range_cm also bounds the flow (518). So no setting keeps the flow and
   removes the rangefinder from the height estimate. Result: STOP at 3e.

b. The idea "send a fixed range Rs, multiply the flow by h / Rs", term by term
   - Flow rate: rate = motion / 200 / dt (rangefinder_lidarmt.c 179). Scaling the motion by h / Rs: works.
   - Gyro: the FC subtracts its gyro in rad/s, which does not depend on height (opticalflow.c 210-211). Only the
     translation part may be scaled; msp_link adds the gyro part after the speed part, so that works.
   - Tilt: the FC uses raw x cos(tilt) (rangefinder.c 360) and x cos(roll), x cos(pitch) (position_estimator.c
     550-551). Send Rs / cos(tilt), as msp_link does now: the tilt terms cancel. The 45 deg cone limit stays.
   - 80 mm and 10 cm minimums (rangefinder_lidarmt.c 43, 170; position_estimator.c 96): any Rs >= 0.1 m passes.
   - Speed resolution: one motion count = Rs / (200 dt) m/s, so a small Rs is finer, not coarser.
   - Height estimate: FAILS. With a fixed Rs the rangefinder term reads 0 change, with 15 times the weight of
     the barometer, so the FC's height stops following the true height and ALT HOLD (which POS HOLD uses in the
     tested switch layout) holds the wrong value. A shifted range (h - constant) keeps the height change right,
     but its first value after arming sets the zero, so the FC height jumps by the shift, and the jump reverses
     when the range stops (Hold loses its fix, or the drone leaves the 0.1-10 m band). Not tested; not added.
